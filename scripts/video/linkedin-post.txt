I’ve been building Live Meeting Notes, an Android app for turning conversations into notes I can return to.

Here’s a first look: live transcription, saved recordings and playback, and easy ways to copy, share, or export notes. Optional AI summaries and action items work with your own provider API key; provider charges may apply.

I’m preparing it for Google Play. The app will be free, with optional purchases for anyone who wants to support development.

The video uses sample meeting content. I’d love to hear what would make this useful in your day-to-day work.
