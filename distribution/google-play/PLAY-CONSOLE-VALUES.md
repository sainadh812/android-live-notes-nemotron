## Pricing

Set up or connect a Google payments profile, then open **Products → App pricing**, select India, and set **₹100**. Review any automatically converted prices before adding other countries. Pricing applies across all release tracks.

Google allows a paid app to become free later. After an app has been offered free, it cannot become paid again under the same package name.

Internal testers can install paid apps without charge. Testers in closed or open testing must purchase paid apps. Because this is a new personal account, the Console will likely require 12 closed testers to remain opted in continuously for 14 days before an application for production access. Keep a record of their feedback because the production-access form asks about it.
