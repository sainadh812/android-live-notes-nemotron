## Full description

Turn conversations into notes you can revisit. Live Meeting Notes brings live transcription, a recording library, and optional AI summaries together on your Android phone.

CAPTURE AND TRANSCRIBE
Follow speech as text while you record. Download a supported speech model for transcription on your phone, or use your device's Android speech recognition service. Choose your phone microphone or a compatible Bluetooth microphone.

REVISIT YOUR RECORDINGS
With a downloaded speech model, save audio alongside your transcript. Play back recordings, change playback speed, and jump through the conversation using transcript timestamps. Timing may be approximate depending on the speech model. Android speech recognition mode saves text only.

KEEP THE TAKEAWAYS
Connect an optional AI provider to generate summaries and action items. OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Qwen connections require your own provider API key and internet access. Provider charges may apply. Once configured, AI summaries send transcript text and prior summary context to your selected provider automatically.

COPY, SHARE, AND EXPORT
Copy your notes, share transcript text or saved audio with an app you choose, and export notes as text files.

CHOOSE HOW SPEECH IS PROCESSED
Downloaded speech models process audio on your phone after download. Android speech recognition may send audio to your device's speech-service provider. Optional AI summaries use the selected provider's online service. Stored notes and settings may be included in Android backup; audio recordings and downloaded models are excluded by the app's backup rules.

Requires Android 8 or later on a supported 64-bit ARM device. Speech models require storage space and an initial download. Transcription and AI results can contain mistakes; review important notes. No Live Meeting Notes account is required.
